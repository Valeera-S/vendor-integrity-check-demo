    About this site

    This is a static copy of the Vendor Integrity Check web form. The full tool runs on a staff workstation, searches each source live, and keeps a copy of every page it reads. The cases here were produced by that tool on the dates shown and copied as they were, including the evidence files and the Word report.

    Vanderbilt University, AI-CPS Lab. Research prototype built for the NYC Department of Design and Construction.

    Every case is a recorded run of public records. Site generated September 30, 2026, 22:27 UTC.
